FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you go solar.

How Much Can I Save with Solar?

It depends on your bill, roof, and when you use power. Most homes size a system to cover most of their daytime use. Book a free site survey for exact numbers.

Will My Solar Work During a Brownout?

A standard grid-tied system switches off during an outage for safety. To keep the lights on during brownouts, add a battery and hybrid inverter so your stored solar power takes over automatically.

What Is Net Metering?

Under the Philippine Net-Metering Program, extra power your panels send to the grid is credited on your electric bill. We help you prepare and file the application with your local utility.

Do You Offer Installation?

We supply the panels and mounting systems. For installation, visit our installation partner link TBA.

What Warranties Do I Get?

Panels carry manufacturer warranties of up to 15 years on the product and 30 years on power output. If you ever need to make a claim, we file and follow it up for you.

Do You Supply Businesses?

Yes. We design and supply systems for homes, stores, offices, and other commercial buildings.
