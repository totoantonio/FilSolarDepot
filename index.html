Why Our Panels

Every panel we carry is bifacial N-type from a recognized global brand, chosen to produce more power from the same roof space.

Power from Both Sides

Bifacial panels also capture sunlight reflected onto their back, adding extra output on light-colored roofs and open ground.

N-Type TOPCon Cells

Newer cell technology with higher efficiency, slower aging, and better performance in hot weather than older P-type panels.

Dual-Glass Durability

Glass on the front and back protects the cells from moisture, heat, and wear for a longer working life.

Long Warranties

Backed by manufacturer warranties of up to 15 years on the product and 30 years on power output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers before you go solar.

How Much Can I Save with Solar?

It depends on your bill, roof, and when you use power. Most homes size a system to cover most of their daytime use. Try the savings calculator above, then book a free site survey for exact numbers.

Will My Solar Work During a Brownout?

A standard grid-tied system switches off during an outage for safety. To keep the lights on during brownouts, add a battery and hybrid inverter so your stored solar power takes over automatically.

What Is Net Metering?

Under the Philippine Net-Metering Program, extra power your panels send to the grid is credited on your electric bill. We help you prepare and file the application with your local utility.

Do You Offer Installation?

What Warranties Do I Get?
